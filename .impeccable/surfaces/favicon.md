# PaperSign favicon

Mode: Operate. This extends the existing identity; no new palette or logo system.
Targets: `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon.ico`,
`public/apple-touch-icon.png`, and `index.html`.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make a PaperSign tab recognizable with the same feather already used
in its navigation, keeping the silhouette readable at 16 pixels.

**OWN-WORLD:** White Lucide feather strokes on the established deep green
`#163f38`, inside a softly rounded square. No initials, gradients or extra marks.

**STORY:** The visitor can locate the signing workspace among browser tabs and
recognize a saved home-screen shortcut.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** One centered feather, generous edge clearance and a strong
green ground. Inspect 16, 32 and 48 pixel icons on light and dark tab surfaces,
alongside the larger touch export.

**FORM:** Direct extension of the existing brand icon; no concept seed applies.
SVG is canonical. ICO contains 16/32/48 pixel frames. The opaque touch PNG lets
the device apply its own corner mask.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
